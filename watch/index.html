Test yourself

Myth or fact?

Make your call, then hear what our guests had to say.

Claim 1 of 3

"A spot check and a full body skin check are the same thing."

Myth

A spot check looks at one mark you are worried about. A full body skin check looks at all of your skin. Say which one you need when you book.

Watch: Spot check vs full body skin check
Claim 2 of 3

"Your test results usually go to your GP before they come to you."

Fact

Results go to the doctor who ordered them, so they can be read alongside your history and explained properly at a follow-up.

Watch: Why your results go to your doctor first
Claim 3 of 3

"Loud music through earbuds, for long periods, can damage your hearing."

Fact

Loud sound over long periods can cause hearing loss that does not come back. Keep the volume moderate and give your ears breaks.

Watch: Are your earbuds damaging your hearing?
